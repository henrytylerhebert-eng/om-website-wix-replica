Behavioral Standards

Community
Expectations.

This community works if you give first. We've built behavioral standards to ensure OM remains a high-trust, high-impact environment.

Give-First Culture

We reward behavior that contributes to the collective growth of the ecosystem. Mentors, partners, and founders are expected to lead with value before seeking it.

High-Trust Engagement

Confidentiality and integrity are non-negotiable. We create a safe space for founders to be vulnerable about their challenges so they can find real solutions.

Direct Feedback

We value clarity over comfort. Feedback at OM is direct, evidence-based, and always aimed at moving the needle for the founder.

What to Expect
  • Access to a network of vetted mentors and connectors.
  • A structured environment for testing and validation.
  • Honest feedback on your business model and progress.
  • Long-term support that grows with your startup.
