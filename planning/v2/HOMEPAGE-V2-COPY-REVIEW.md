# Homepage 2.0 page contract and copy review

**Status:** Draft only  
**Copy goal:** Clarity  
**Primary audience:** People considering or building early-stage technology, research-driven, and innovative companies.  
**Primary action:** `Book a call` to an owner-confirmed destination.  
**Implementation status:** Planning artifact only. The candidate HTML has not been revised to this contract.

## Strategic objective

Help a founder recognize that Opportunity Machine may be relevant, understand what is confirmed about the organization, and choose one low-friction next step without mistaking an invitation for admission, funding, or guaranteed access.

## Visitor questions

1. Is this for someone like me?
2. What kind of help could I find here?
3. What would I actually experience?
4. Why should I trust this organization?
5. What should I do next?

## Recommended hierarchy

1. Protected founder-recognition hero.
2. Economic-development identity and no-equity statement.
3. Need-based routing before program vocabulary.
4. Confirmed support explanation.
5. Attributed founder proof.
6. Regional-purpose bridge.
7. One human invitation.

This uses the peer-research patterns for need-based routing, regional purpose, and a human starting point as hypotheses only. The supplied studies do not show that these patterns increase conversion.

## Copy review

### 1. Hero: locked

**Video:** Approved OM hero video, YouTube ID `yk1Ij-wW_P0`  
**Fixed text:** `For founders who…`  
**Rotation:**

- `want experienced guidance and advice.`
- `want to meet and build relationships with other founders.`
- `want clearer insight into what they’re missing.`
- `want to build real connections with investors.`
- `want to get farther, faster.`

**Primary CTA:** `Book a call`  
**Destination:** `Unknown`  
**Rule:** No support line, second CTA, stat, or diagnostic link inside the hero.

### 2. Opening identity: exact approved claims

> Opportunity Machine is an economic-development organization.
>
> OM takes no equity.

This is intentionally direct. It does not claim government-agency status, free participation, guaranteed ownership, funding, or measured economic impact.

### 3. Need-based routing: proposed

**Heading:** `Start with what you need.`

- `I have an idea.`
- `I am building.`
- `I am growing.`
- `I need technical help.`
- `I am not sure where to begin.`

Each route must lead to a confirmed explanation or the primary conversation action. It must not infer eligibility or promise access.

### 4. Support explanation: blocked copy slot

The current candidate lists coaching, mentorship, customer discovery, Builder programming, workspace, peer relationships, introductions, experiments, and business-model work. That combined service statement is not cleared as one current public promise.

Before writing this section, confirm:

- Current programs and cohort availability
- Builder cost, timing, eligibility, and current format
- Mentor access
- Membership and workspace access, price, and hours
- Technical-support routes
- What an introduction means and when OM offers one

The page structure can be built, but this public copy slot remains blocked.

### 5. Founder proof: approved media, narrow framing

**Proposed heading:** `Hear from founders.`

Approved video assets:

- GlowSens
- Mallard Bay
- Keepers

The videos may be embedded. Their presence does not clear surrounding claims about funding, growth, jobs, typical outcomes, or OM causation.

If the GlowSens pull quote is used, preserve its approved source text exactly:

> The building is great, but 90% of the value is the people - because the people are what make it valuable.

Do not silently replace the hyphen or alter the attribution.

### 6. Regional purpose: proposed, approval required

**Heading:** `Why this work matters here`

Keep this section short until an approved economic-development boilerplate exists. It may explain OM's purpose, but it cannot claim job creation, company growth, capital formation, retention, or regional impact without separately defined and approved measures.

### 7. Final invitation: proposed

**Heading:** `Tell us what you're building.`

**CTA:** `Book a call`

Do not promise a 20-minute call, a response time, a program match, an introduction, or mentor access until the booking workflow and operating process are confirmed.
