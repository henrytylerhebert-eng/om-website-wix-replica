A strong design direction does not clear a claim for publication.

## Global experience

The site speaks to founders first, then makes Membership the persistent conversion route. OM is a connector within the Louisiana startup ecosystem, not a gatekeeper. Start with a visitor's situation, explain the appropriate route, show only cleared proof, and end with a useful next action.

### Navigation

`Membership` · `Programs` · `Events` · `Mentors` · `Alumni` · `Ecosystem` · `About OM`

`Apply for membership` and `Schedule a call` are the intended repeated actions. Their exact destinations and post-click process remain to be confirmed before live implementation. Community is folded into Ecosystem for this version; Talent/ETI belongs inside Programs or Ecosystem, not the main navigation.

### Shared visual and layout direction

- Remove the closure-for-private-event banner from the public site.
- Use a readable founder-led hero with an image/video treatment that preserves text contrast. Remove the light-blue wash; use a subtle dark gradient behind copy only when necessary.
- Use cards for choices: program stages, program offerings, membership benefits, board members, story/video entries, and logo groups.
- Keep cards spacious, left-aligned, and consistent. Do not use cards as decorative containers for unverified outcomes.
- Let ecosystem and event organizations retain their own logos. Do not turn every group into a uniform blue-tile treatment.
- Keep OM's mission and institutional purpose on About; make `OM takes no equity.` visible at Membership and other participation decisions without inferring no cost or guaranteed ownership.

## Homepage

**Visitor:** A founder who is stuck, uncertain, or trying to make a next decision.
**Goal:** Recognition and a clear route, not a long Builder sales page.

```
Hero: founder pain, large and primary
├── Supporting label: For founders who...
├── Rotating/secondary needs: guidance, founder relationships,
│   clearer insight, investor connections, and getting further faster
└── Action: Schedule a call or Apply for membership, once destination is confirmed
     ↓
Short founder-recognition section
     ↓
Programs and Membership routes
     ↓
Current-member story/video proof, only when cleared
     ↓
Ecosystem connection and final conversion action
```

**Hero copy direction:** Retire `TEMP HERO ... FINAL COPY COMING` and the claim that most founders spend a year building before anyone will buy. The main thought is that founders can get stuck trying to understand why customers will not buy. It needs a concise, compassionate final line; do not lecture them for being stuck.

`For founders who...` is a smaller supporting label, not the primary headline. The recap uses “get further faster”; the currently protected August record uses “get farther, faster.” Treat that spelling change as an exact-copy decision for Tyler before changing a live hero.

**Cards:** Do not lead with five generic pain cards and a long Builder sequence. Use a small number of clear routes: explore Membership, explore Programs, talk with OM, and see founder stories. A need-router can still appear below the hero, but it must support the Membership-first conversion direction.

**Peer-pattern rationale:** P02 and P08 support routing by a visitor question and giving an uncertain visitor a human start. They do not establish that a particular hero line or card count converts better.

## Programs portfolio

**Visitor:** A founder seeking the right kind of support for their stage.
**Goal:** Replace a single Builder-first page with a portfolio that explains what OM can address across stages.

```
Programs H1: built for every stage of growth
     ↓
Three stage cards: Visionary · Early Mover · Scaler
     ↓
Program and service cards, grouped by the founder need they support
     ↓
Cohort experience section
     ↓
Program detail pages and a conversion action
```

### Stage cards

- **Visionary:** A person with a problem space, domain knowledge, or early concept who needs a clearer starting point.
- **Early Mover:** A person with a live product or early users who needs structure for validation and traction.
- **Scaler:** A person with revenue, capital, or market movement who needs to refine unit economics and scale.

These are plain-language navigation devices, not eligibility tiers or promises. Each card expands or links to the relevant confirmed program/service page.

### Portfolio cards

The proposed inventory includes Builder 1.0, Builder 2.0, Mentor Program, ETI, a tech-transfer/LTTO item, Pitch Prep, Beta Test Plans or Reports, AI-native programming, and relevant events. Mark the inventory `pending program-owner confirmation` until the official Programs-versus-Services list and live availability are supplied.

Do not publish session-by-session curriculum on the website. It goes stale, makes visitors self-select out too early, and is not needed to understand the program. Use high-level frameworks and what a participant will work through instead.

### Builder detail pages

- **Builder 1.0:** Fundamental frameworks that remain useful from idea through growth: customer discovery, jobs-to-be-done, early traction thinking, and a basis for later research/tool use.
- **Builder 2.0:** A deeper extension focused on unit economics, customer acquisition strategy, AI-native workflows and automations, and an alumni re-engagement path.

These are strategy descriptions. Current dates, price, cohort format, prerequisites, session content, and enrollment rules remain Unknown until the program owner confirms them.

### Cohort experience

Place one dedicated Cohort Experience section on Programs, then echo its most relevant sentence on Builder detail pages. Describe peer support, shared wins, accountability, and a growing network in concrete, non-judgmental language. Do not use the CrossFit analogy verbatim as public copy unless Tyler chooses it.

**Peer-pattern rationale:** P04 supports a consistent, comparable program presentation. It does not authorize terms, requirements, or outcomes.

The first version needs Tyler's named organizations, signature events, partners, relationship labels, logo permission, and official destination links. **Peer-pattern rationale:** P06 supports explaining each regional relationship's concrete role. It does not authorize a copied directory or partner claim.
