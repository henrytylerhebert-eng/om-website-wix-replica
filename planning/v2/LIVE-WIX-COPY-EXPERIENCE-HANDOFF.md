## The global experience to apply

```
Visitor need
   ↓
Plain-language route or one clear page purpose
   ↓
Specific explanation of the support or next decision
   ↓
Claim-matched proof only when cleared
   ↓
One action with an understood destination and next step
```

### Navigation and shell

Replace program-name-first navigation with a founder-readable shell:

`Start Here` · `Programs` · `Mentors and Experts` · `Technical Support` · `Community and Events` · `Workspace and Membership` · `Founder Stories` · `About` · `Book a call`

`Ecosystem` becomes a supporting page rather than a primary founder route. Link directory needs to LA.IO rather than maintain a second directory. Keep one stable CTA label, `Book a call`, but do not claim a booking result or response time until its destination and handoff are confirmed.

### Card system

Use cards only where a visitor is choosing among real paths or comparing a defined set. Every card needs: a founder question, a short explanation, one honest action, and a status-aware destination. Avoid cards that merely restate brand language or use an outcome as a promise.

| Card type | Used on | Required content | Do not include until confirmed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Need route | Home | “I have an idea to test,” “I need technical support,” and similar plain-language needs | Admission, availability, or immediate access |
| Route comparison | Technical Support, Builder, Membership | Who it is for, what work it covers, how to learn more | Price, dates, eligibility, mentor access, or hours |
| Expertise filter | Mentors | Challenge or expertise area | A roster beyond cleared profiles, unrestricted access, response times |
| Proof card | Founder Stories, Home | Cleared video or claim with plain attribution | Funding, revenue, outcomes, or quotes without individual clearance |
| Event card | Community and Events | Date, audience, format, location, cost, action | Placeholder dates, past figures framed as current, unverified metrics |

## How the skills make each decision

- **Website Strategist:** separates live-page observation from approved copy and source truth.
- **Page Architect:** defines each page's one primary visitor question, ordered modules, card type, action, and open gates.
- **Peer Pattern Finder:** uses the submitted 152-site study as the primary pattern source and the 174-site report only as supplemental discovery. It does not copy a peer's visual system, operations, or claims.
- **Visitor Router and Journey Auditor:** test whether a founder can recognize relevance, find a supported route, and understand what happens after the click. This is a synthetic desk review, not user research.
- **Positioning Guard and Proof Auditor:** retain early economic-development/no-equity context while removing unsupported fee, access, outcome, testimonial, metric, and causal language.
- **Web Copy Builder:** drafts only bounded modules after a page contract is accepted. Each new module is kept short and explicitly marked candidate until approved.
