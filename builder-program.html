Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a finished idea?

No. Half-formed is normal. The program exists to form it.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Over 70% of the founders we work with don't have technical backgrounds — and OM's Technical Support Programs exist to bridge exactly that gap.

What if I find out my idea doesn't work?

That's a real outcome we're proud of. You leave knowing your first idea wasn't it — with your savings intact and a sharper eye for the next one.

How much time does it take?

Ten weeks: weekly working sessions with your cohort, plus real customer conversations between sessions.

What happens on the interest call?

A twenty-minute conversation, not a test. Tell us where you are — we'll tell you honestly whether Builder is the right next step, and point you to what is if it isn't.
