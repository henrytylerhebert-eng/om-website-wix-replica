How This Actually Works

Founders don't guess here. They ask, and they listen.

Every Builder cohort runs on the same discipline: talk to real customers before you build anything. Founders leave their first few sessions with a list of people to call — not a to-do list for their product. By the middle of the program, they're making decisions from what those conversations actually told them, not from what they assumed going in. That's the whole method. No secret framework, no jargon. Ask the people who'd actually pay you. Listen for what they say and what they don't. Build the thing that survives that conversation.

This is what every cohort does, in every session — not a highlight reel from one founder, a discipline every founder here practices.
